# FeedbackHub — Use Cases

### Solo founders
- **Pain:** Notes scattered across Slack/DMs.
- **How FeedbackHub helps:** Cluster into themes with frequency + build-next pick.
### Indie hackers
- **Pain:** No time for a full research process.
- **How FeedbackHub helps:** Paste lines; get buckets in minutes.
### Small PMs
- **Pain:** Need a shareable theme summary.
- **How FeedbackHub helps:** Export themes for roadmap reviews.
### Support → product
- **Pain:** Tickets hide repeated pain.
- **How FeedbackHub helps:** Bucket bugs vs pricing vs UX.
