# FeedbackHub — Security & Compliance

> **MANDATORY module** for this product site.

## What we handle
user feedback lines you paste for theme clustering. Avoid pasting personal data you should not process.

## Data handling commitments
- Inputs run the product pipeline and are retained only as long as needed for your audit log (paid tiers) or until you delete the run.
- We apply access controls consistent with **GDPR Art. 32** (security of processing) where personal data is processed.
- BYOK keys (Enterprise), when offered, are stored **server-side only** and never exposed to the browser.
- Payments are processed by **Waffo Pancake** (merchant of record).

## Our compliance posture (honesty rule)
- FeedbackHub is **decision-support**, not a law firm, clinic, or certified auditor.
- FeedbackHub clusters themes from text you provide. It does not guarantee the right roadmap, 100% perfect sentiment, or that you will never miss a theme. Do not fabricate user testimonials from outputs.
- We do **NOT** use the words guarantee / 100% / never miss as efficacy claims.
- For binding advice, consult a qualified professional in the relevant domain.
